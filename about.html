Our Story

Bridging therapy and school.

Willow started with something we kept noticing in our work with neurodivergent children. We would see children make beautiful progress in therapy, but that progress didn’t always follow them into school, home, relationships, or everyday life. It made us realize that therapy cannot exist on its own. Children need the people and environments around them to be connected. That is why we created Willow—to build a bridge between therapy, school, home, and everyday life, while looking at the child as a whole. We want to support not just what a child can do, but how they communicate, learn, connect, regulate, become independent, and experience the world.

For us, meaningful progress is when what a child learns becomes part of their everyday life.

"Our dream is simple: to make sure no parents, child, or teacher feels alone in this journey." — Willow TDC
