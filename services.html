Therapy plans as unique as your child.

Eight specialties, one connected team. Explore what each therapy involves and who it tends to help most.

Speech-Language Therapy

Our speech-language pathologists work on everything from early sound development to complex conversation, reading, and social language skills.

  • Articulation, fluency, and voice support
  • Language comprehension and expression
  • Augmentative & alternative communication (AAC)
  • Social communication and pragmatics

Occupational Therapy

OT helps children build the fine motor, sensory processing, and self-care skills they need for school and everyday independence.

  • Handwriting and fine motor coordination
  • Sensory processing and regulation
  • Self-care skills: dressing, feeding, grooming
  • Visual-motor and executive function skills
  • Toilet training and ADL (Activities of Daily Living) support

Physical Therapy

Our physical therapists support gross motor development, from early crawling and walking to sports-ready strength and balance.

  • Strength, balance, and coordination
  • Gait training and mobility support
  • Post-injury or post-surgical recovery
  • Gross motor milestone development

Behavioral & Psychological Intervention

Individualized, evidence-based behavioral support helps children build routines, coping tools, and positive social behavior.

  • Cognitive & Learning — Attention, memory, processing, learning, systemic desentization and executive functioning
  • Emotional & Behavioral — Affect, emotional regulation, behavioral regulation, and adaptive functioning.
  • Social & Interpersonal — Social cognition, communication, relationships, and social functioning.
  • Parent training and home carryover

Feeding Therapy

We help children who struggle with picky eating, texture aversions, or mealtime anxiety build a calmer relationship with food.

  • Texture and food-variety expansion
  • Oral-motor strength for chewing and swallowing
  • Mealtime routine and behavior support
  • Family coaching for calmer mealtimes

Early Intervention (Ages 0–4)

Focused, family-guided support during the years when the brain is developing fastest — often in your own home.

  • Developmental screening and monitoring
  • In-home coaching for caregivers
  • Early communication and motor foundations
  • Smooth transition planning into preschool services

Social Skills Groups

Small, therapist-guided peer groups give kids a low-pressure place to practice friendship and communication skills.

  • Turn-taking and conversation skills
  • Reading social cues and body language
  • Conflict resolution and flexible thinking
  • Confidence-building in group settings

Developmental Assessments

A comprehensive evaluation gives you clear answers and a specific, actionable plan — not just a diagnosis.

  • Speech, motor, and cognitive screening
  • Sensory and behavioral profile
  • Written report in plain, parent-friendly language
  • A recommended plan across every needed service

Not sure which therapy fits?

Start with a developmental assessment and we'll map out the right combination together.
