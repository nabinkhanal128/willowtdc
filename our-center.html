Where every space is part of the learning.

A closer look at how Willow's environment is designed — and why your child's day here moves between spaces on purpose.

Our Environment

A Space Designed for Development

At Willow, we believe that the environment is an important part of a child's learning and development. Our center is designed as a child-centered, engaging and responsive environment where children can explore, learn, communicate and develop skills across different areas.

Inspired by the principles of the Reggio Emilia approach, we view the environment as an active part of the learning process. Each space, activity and interaction is intentionally designed to encourage curiosity, independence, communication and meaningful participation.

How Sessions Work

Learning Through Stations

Rather than spending the entire day in one room or with one intervention, children at Willow experience station rotation. Throughout their session, children move through different learning and intervention stations according to their individualized goals, developmental needs and program plan.

A child will experience:

Cognitive & Learning Station

Activities targeting attention, memory, problem-solving, pre-academic skills and cognitive development.

Communication Station

Opportunities to develop functional communication, language, comprehension and social communication.

Creative & Expressive Station

Art, sensory and creative activities that encourage self-expression, exploration and fine-motor development.

Social & Emotional Station

Activities supporting emotional regulation, social interaction, turn-taking, flexibility and relationship-building.

Movement & Play Station

Structured and unstructured play opportunities supporting motor development, coordination, body awareness and functional play.

Learning & Skill-Building Station

Individualized activities supporting academic readiness, functional learning, independence and everyday skills.

Why It Matters

One Child, Multiple Learning Experiences

Station rotation allows children to experience different environments, activities and professionals within the same center. It also creates opportunities to work on multiple developmental goals throughout the day while keeping learning active, varied and engaging.

Each child's rotation is planned according to their individual goals and current developmental profile.

"The stations are not simply different rooms — they are interconnected parts of one individualized developmental program."

Our Philosophy

The Environment as a Teacher

At Willow, we intentionally design our spaces to invite children to explore, participate and learn. From our cognitive classroom and therapy spaces to our creative areas and play environments, every space has a purpose.

We don't just provide therapy in a room. We create an environment where development can happen throughout the day.
